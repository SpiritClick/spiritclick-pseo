The Tower reversed

A needed change arriving more gently, or being delayed. Reversed, The Tower can show a change you have been postponing, or a disruption unfolding in slower motion. It may also mark the aftershock period, when things feel unsteady but the worst has passed. Rebuild only on what is actually true.

The Tower in love

In love, The Tower often brings a truth to the surface that changes the shape of a relationship. Painful or not, honesty here clears ground that pretending never could.

The Tower for work and money

At work, The Tower can mean a sudden restructuring, a lost client, or a plan scrapped at the last minute. Keep your documents and your options close while things settle.
