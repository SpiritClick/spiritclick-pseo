Ace of Cups reversed

An emotional cup that needs refilling first. Reversed, the Ace of Cups suggests feelings are dammed up, drained, or hard to trust right now. The capacity for joy is not gone, it is tired. Quiet, rest, and one honest conversation tend to reopen the flow better than forcing cheer.

Reversed keywords: blocked feelings, emptiness, holding back, overwhelm.

Ace of Cups in love

In love, the Ace of Cups is one of the sweetest cards in the deck, new love or a renewal of tenderness in an existing bond. It favors saying the soft thing out loud.

Ace of Cups for work and money

At work, the Ace of Cups favors work that feels meaningful and creative projects that come from the heart. Morale and goodwill are genuine resources under this card.
