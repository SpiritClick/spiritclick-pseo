Six of Swords Meaning

Moving toward calmer water, one steady mile at a time. The Six of Swords is the journey from rough water toward calmer ground. It rarely happens overnight, and you may carry some of the old weight with you, but the direction is right. Quiet, steady progress is the whole point of this card.

Six of Swords reversed

A transition resisted or baggage carried along. Reversed, the Six of Swords suggests a move or change you are resisting, or old trouble being packed along for the trip. It can also show a transition taking longer than hoped. Unpack one old thing at a time.

Reversed keywords: resistance, unfinished business, slow transition, carrying the past.

Six of Swords in love

In love, the Six of Swords can show a couple moving past a rough patch together, or one person gently leaving a hard chapter behind. Distance from old drama helps love breathe.

Six of Swords for work and money

At work, the Six of Swords favors changing teams, tools, or routines to escape a draining setup. It can also mark travel for work or a calmer role ahead.

Advice and yes or no

Keep moving toward the calmer version, even slowly. Handle the practical steps of the move first, one per day.
