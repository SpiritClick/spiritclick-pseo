Six of Cups Meaning

Sweet memories and simple kindness returning. The Six of Cups carries the warmth of memory. It can bring reunions, old friends resurfacing, or simple pleasures that remind you of who you were before life got complicated. Kindness, given and received plainly, is the currency of this card.

Six of Cups reversed

Living in the past, or ready to leave it kindly. Reversed, the Six of Cups suggests the past has been getting more of your present than it should. It can also mark a healthy release, keeping the good memories and letting the old arrangement finally stay finished. Either way, your life is in front of you.

Reversed keywords: stuck in the past, releasing old ties, growing up.

Six of Cups in love

In love, the Six of Cups can bring an old flame or an old friend back into view, or simply a sweeter, simpler phase with a partner. Judge the present person, not the memory.

Six of Cups for work and money

At work, the Six of Cups favors returning to a skill, place, or collaborator from earlier in your path. Work with children, teaching, and sharing what you know also fit this card.

Advice and yes or no

Take the good memory with you and keep walking forward. Do one simple thing you loved years ago and let it be enough.

Yes or no: Yes - with warmth, if the past is not running it.

Also: The Six of Cups can also highlight children and the healing that comes from simple, unguarded joy.
