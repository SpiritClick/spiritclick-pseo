Fair judgment built on facts and experience. The King of Swords rules by reason, fairness, and clear standards. He favors decisions made on evidence, advice from real experts, and rules applied evenly to everyone. Under this card, integrity in how you decide matters as much as the decision.

King of Swords reversed

Judgment clouded or authority used coldly. Reversed, the King of Swords suggests judgment clouded by bias, or authority used in a cold or self-serving way. It can also show overthinking a decision that the facts already answer. Return to the evidence and the standard you would want applied to you.

King of Swords in love

In love, the King of Swords values honesty, loyalty, and a partner who thinks as clearly as they feel. It favors solving problems together with words instead of silence or storms.
