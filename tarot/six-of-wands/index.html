Six of Wands reversed

A win that feels smaller than expected. Reversed, the Six of Wands can show recognition that is delayed, shared unevenly, or quieter than you hoped. It can also flag pride getting in the way of the next step. The work still counts even when the applause is late.

Six of Wands in love

In love, the Six of Wands is feeling chosen and appreciated out loud. It favors couples who champion each other in public, not just in private.
