Five of Cups Meaning

Grieving what spilled, with more standing than you think. The Five of Cups honors real disappointment. Something did not work out, and pretending otherwise helps no one. But the card is careful to show that not everything is lost, and a bridge back exists when you are ready to turn around and look.

Five of Cups reversed

Turning toward what remains, and healing. Reversed, the Five of Cups marks the turn toward healing. Regret loosens, lessons settle in, and what remains starts to look like enough to build on. It favors forgiving yourself for the part you keep replaying.

Reversed keywords: acceptance, healing, moving forward, lessons learned.

Five of Cups in love

In love, the Five of Cups can show heartbreak or disappointment being carried into the present. It asks for honest grieving, and for noticing who or what is still standing beside you.

Five of Cups for work and money

At work, the Five of Cups points to a setback, a lost client, or a project that fell short. Review it once for the lesson, then give your energy to what is still viable.
