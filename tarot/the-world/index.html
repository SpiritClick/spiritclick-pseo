The World reversed

One last piece before the finish. Reversed, The World shows you are close but not quite finished. A loose end, a final form, or one honest conversation may be all that stands between you and closure. Finish it rather than starting something new on top of it.

The World in love

In love, The World is a relationship that feels complete and mutually giving, or a personal chapter of healing that leaves you genuinely ready for love. It favors wholeness over need.

The World for work and money

At work, The World marks a project delivered, a goal reached, or recognition that travels further than your usual circle. It is also a good card for work that reaches people at a distance.
