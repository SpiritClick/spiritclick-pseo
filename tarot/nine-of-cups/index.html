Nine of Cups Meaning

Contentment, and a wish with real weight behind it. The Nine of Cups is the wish card, and the quieter gift underneath it is contentment. It shows satisfaction with what you have built and a wish that has a real chance because you know what it actually is. Enjoy the good stretch without apologizing for it.

Nine of Cups reversed

A wish that needs defining, or satisfaction that slips. Reversed, the Nine of Cups suggests a wish that stays fuzzy, or pleasures that satisfy for an evening and echo hollow after. It asks you to define what enough actually looks like for you. A clearer wish is far easier to move toward.

Reversed keywords: unfulfilled wishes, overindulgence, vague wanting, smugness.

Nine of Cups in love

In love, the Nine of Cups is feeling emotionally satisfied and genuinely wanted. For singles it favors confidence and knowing your worth before choosing who gets close.

Nine of Cups for work and money

At work, the Nine of Cups marks a satisfying result and the confidence that comes with it. It is a fine card for enjoying your rates, your clients, and your own standards.
