Knight of Swords reversed

Haste outrunning good judgment. Reversed, the Knight of Swords warns of rushing in, arguing for the thrill of it, or scattering energy across five fights at once. Slow down enough to aim. A paused charge often lands better than a wild one.

Reversed keywords: haste, bluntness, scattered charge, arguments.

Knight of Swords in love

In love, the Knight of Swords favors direct conversation and clearing the air quickly. Watch the delivery, though, because a true thing said harshly still lands harshly.

Knight of Swords for work and money

At work, the Knight of Swords suits deadlines, launches, debates, and fixing an urgent problem head-on. Channel the speed into one priority.

Advice and yes or no
