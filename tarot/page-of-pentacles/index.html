Page of Pentacles reversed

A good plan stalled at the starting line. Reversed, the Page of Pentacles suggests a good plan stuck in the someday pile, or details missed because attention wandered. It can also show a delay in expected news. Return to step one and simply start it.

Page of Pentacles in love

In love, the Page of Pentacles favors showing interest through reliability, remembering the small things, showing up when promised. Slow and steady wins real trust here.
