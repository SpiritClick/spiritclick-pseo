King of Cups Meaning

Feelings mastered, kindness in charge. The King of Cups has a full heart and a steady hand on it. He feels deeply without being swept away, listens before he answers, and is the person others steady themselves against. This card favors calm honesty, generous listening, and decisions that account for feelings without being ruled by them.

King of Cups reversed

Feelings managed a little too tightly. Reversed, the King of Cups can show feelings kept under such tight control that they leak out sideways as distance or sudden moods. It may also flag someone whose warmth turns on and off strategically. Let a trusted person see the unedited version occasionally.

King of Cups in love

In love, the King of Cups is a mature, emotionally available partner who shows love steadily rather than theatrically. It favors relationships where hard feelings can be discussed without storms.

King of Cups for work and money

At work, the King of Cups favors leadership in tense rooms, negotiation, and any role involving trust and counsel. Your calm under pressure is a professional asset people remember.
