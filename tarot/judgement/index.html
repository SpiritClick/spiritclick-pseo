Judgement Meaning

A clear-eyed reckoning leads to a fresh start. Judgement is the moment of honest review. It asks you to look at the whole story, yours included, without flinching or exaggerating, and then rise to what it asks of you. Many people meet this card when a real calling or a big decision can no longer be postponed.

Judgement reversed

A call you keep not answering. Reversed, Judgement suggests a needed reckoning is being avoided, or that self criticism has grown louder than the facts. It can also show fear of being judged holding you back. The review is kinder, and more useful, than the dread of it.

Reversed keywords: avoidance, self doubt, unfinished reckoning, harsh judgment.

Judgement in love

In love, Judgement favors honest stock-taking, forgiveness where it is earned, and deciding whether a relationship gets a genuine fresh start. Old scores kept in a drawer help no one.

Judgement for work and money

At work, Judgement can mark a performance reckoning, a calling toward different work, or results finally being tallied. It favors owning your record and applying its lessons.
