Knight of Wands reversed

Charging ahead without a plan for landing. Reversed, the Knight of Wands warns of rushing in for the thrill and losing interest at the first obstacle. It can also show restlessness mistaken for direction. Channel the fire into one target and stay for the unglamorous middle part.

Knight of Wands in love

In love, the Knight of Wands is intense attraction and fast movement. It favors honesty about whether this is a spark or a build, because the two are enjoyed differently.
