Temperance reversed

Too much of one thing, not enough of another. Reversed, Temperance points to imbalance, overdoing one area while another starves. It can show impatience with a process that simply needs more time. Small corrections now prevent a bigger swing later.

Temperance in love

In love, Temperance is two different people learning each other's rhythms. It favors compromise that neither person resents, and healing after a rough patch at a realistic pace.

Temperance for work and money

At work, Temperance supports teamwork between different styles, careful budgeting of energy, and projects that blend skills. Avoid the all-or-nothing push.
