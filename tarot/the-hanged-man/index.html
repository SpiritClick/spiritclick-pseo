The Hanged Man reversed

Waiting that no longer serves you. Reversed, The Hanged Man suggests the wait has stopped teaching you anything. It can show stalling, feeling stuck in sacrifice mode, or resisting a release you know is needed. Ask what the pause is for, and if there is no answer, it may be time to move.

Reversed keywords: stalling, martyrdom, restlessness, wasted wait.

The Hanged Man in love

In love, The Hanged Man often marks a relationship in a holding pattern. Use the pause to see your partner and yourself more honestly, rather than forcing a verdict.

The Hanged Man for work and money

At work, The Hanged Man advises waiting out a delay instead of forcing a half-ready launch. A fresh angle on an old problem is the real prize here.
