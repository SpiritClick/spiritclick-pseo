Death Meaning

An ending that makes room for what is next. Death marks the close of a chapter so a new one can begin. It is rarely about literal death, it is about a role, habit, or arrangement that has run its course. Letting it end cleanly frees real energy for what comes next.

Death reversed

Holding on to a chapter that has closed. Reversed, Death shows resistance to an ending you already know is real. Clinging to the old shape of things tends to drain more than the goodbye itself would. Small, honest steps toward closure work better than one dramatic break.

Reversed keywords: resistance, stagnation, unfinished goodbye, fear of change.

Death in love

In love, Death can mark the end of a phase, a pattern, or sometimes a relationship itself. What matters is whether both people are willing to let the old version go and build something truer.

Death for work and money

At work, Death points to a job, project, or method that has finished its run. Clearing it out makes space and focus for the next thing.
