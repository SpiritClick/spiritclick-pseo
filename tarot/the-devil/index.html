The Devil Meaning

A pattern that has more hold on you than it should. The Devil names the habit, attachment, or arrangement that quietly runs the show. It is not about evil, it is about honesty, seeing where a pleasure, a fear, or an old bargain has become a chain. Naming it plainly is the first loosening.

The Devil reversed

Loosening the grip of an old pattern. Reversed, The Devil is a hopeful card, it shows the grip weakening. It marks recognizing the pattern, asking for support, or walking away from a deal that cost too much. Freedom here is built one decision at a time.

Reversed keywords: breaking free, awareness, recovery, letting go.

The Devil in love

In love, The Devil asks honest questions about attachment versus connection. It can flag jealousy, old wounds replaying, or staying for comfort rather than care, and it favors truth over drama.

The Devil for work and money

At work, The Devil can point to a job kept only out of fear, or spending and workload habits that own you. It asks what the arrangement really costs you.
