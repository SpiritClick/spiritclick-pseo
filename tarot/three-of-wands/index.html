Three of Wands reversed

Delays on something already set in motion. Reversed, the Three of Wands points to delays, shipping that has not arrived, or plans meeting more resistance than expected. The direction may still be right even when the timing slips. Check the details, then give it a fair window before you judge it.

Reversed keywords: delays, obstacles, frustration, narrow view.

Three of Wands in love

In love, the Three of Wands can show a connection growing across distance, or two people building toward a shared future. Progress is real even when it is not instant.

Three of Wands for work and money

At work, the Three of Wands favors growth, new markets, and work that reaches further than your usual circle. It is a good card for launches already underway.
