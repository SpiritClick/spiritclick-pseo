The Sun reversed

Joy delayed, not denied. Reversed, The Sun suggests happiness that feels slightly out of reach, a good thing delayed, or energy running lower than usual. The warmth is still there, it just needs a smaller doorway. Simple pleasures tend to restart it.

Reversed keywords: temporary gloom, overexposure, forced cheer, low energy.

The Sun in love

In love, The Sun is open, joyful, and uncomplicated connection. It favors couples who can be fully themselves together, and new love that feels easy from the start.

The Sun for work and money

At work, The Sun favors visibility, recognition, and projects launched in the open. It is a strong card for sharing good results and leading with confidence.
