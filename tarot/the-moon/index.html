The Moon Meaning

Things are not fully clear yet, and that is the point. The Moon moves through fog. Facts may be missing, stories may not match, and feelings run strong on partial information. This card asks you to move slowly, trust your instincts about people, and wait for daylight before big commitments.

The Moon reversed

Confusion lifting as facts come to light. Reversed, The Moon shows the fog beginning to lift. Confusion eases, a misunderstanding clears, or a fear loses its grip once it is looked at directly. What surfaces now can be worked with.

The Moon in love

In love, The Moon asks you to separate real signals from anxious guessing. If something feels hidden, ask the calm question instead of building a case in the dark.

The Moon for work and money

At work, The Moon warns against signing or deciding on incomplete information. Double-check the details, read the fine print, and confirm what was actually said.

Advice and yes or no

Wait for more light before you commit. Write the worry down and check it against facts in the morning.
