Ace of Swords reversed

Confusion clouding a clear answer. Reversed, the Ace of Swords suggests muddled information, crossed wires, or words used more sharply than intended. Slow down, check the facts, and let tempers cool before you decide what is true.

Ace of Swords in love

In love, the Ace of Swords favors one honest conversation over weeks of guessing. Say the true thing kindly and listen for the true thing back.
