The Hermit reversed

Withdrawal that has gone on too long. Reversed, The Hermit suggests solitude has tipped into isolation, or that reflection has become a way to avoid a needed conversation. It can also show returning to the world after a needed pause. Either way, connection is the missing piece.

Reversed keywords: isolation, avoidance, overthinking, feeling cut off.

The Hermit in love

In love, The Hermit often marks a need for space to think, yours or theirs. It is not rejection, it is processing, and it works best when named out loud.

The Hermit for work and money

At work, The Hermit favors independent study, deep focus, and reviewing a plan before sharing it. Mentoring others from experience also fits this card.
