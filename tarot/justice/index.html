Justice Meaning

Fair dealing and clear facts settle the matter. Justice asks for honesty, fairness, and decisions based on facts rather than feelings in the moment. It favors contracts reviewed carefully, debts acknowledged, and credit given where it is due. What is fair tends to win under this card.

Justice reversed

An imbalance asking to be corrected. Reversed, Justice points to an imbalance, a misunderstanding, or a situation where someone is not getting a fair hearing. It can also show avoiding responsibility for a choice. Correcting the record early prevents a bigger cost later.

Justice in love

In love, Justice favors fairness and equal effort between partners. It asks hard questions about balance, honesty, and whether both people are truly heard.

Justice for work and money

At work, Justice supports negotiations, reviews, and legal or paperwork matters handled by the book. Document things and keep receipts.
