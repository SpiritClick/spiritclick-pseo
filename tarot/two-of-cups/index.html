Two of Cups reversed

A bond out of balance or a talk postponed. Reversed, the Two of Cups points to a partnership where the effort or honesty has gone uneven. It can also show a needed conversation being postponed until it grows. Restore balance with one direct, kind talk rather than scorekeeping.

Two of Cups in love

In love, the Two of Cups is mutual, respectful, and real. For new connections it is a strong sign of genuine compatibility, and for couples it favors reconnecting as a team.

Two of Cups for work and money

At work, the Two of Cups favors partnerships, trusted colleagues, and agreements built on respect. Choose collaborators whose values match yours, not only their resumes.
