Earth grounds air; air lightens earth. The pairing works when each respects what the other is actually built from.

Capricorn in love

In love, Capricorn is slow, serious, and quietly devoted. They prove love by building a life, fixing problems, and staying. Capricorn needs respect, patience, and a partner who understands that work is not the rival, it is the foundation.

Aquarius in love

In love, Aquarius starts with friendship and intellectual fit. They fall for a mind and a worldview before anything else. Aquarius needs autonomy, interesting conversation, and freedom from emotional scripts.

Where this pair is strong

Where it rubs
