The short answer

Fire brings the spark and the appetite; earth brings the staying power and the plan. Together they can build something exciting that also lasts.

Virgo in love

In love, Virgo trusts slowly and loves practically. Acts of service are the love language; grand gestures matter less than reliability. Virgo needs respect, sincerity, and room to process before big emotional conversations.

Sagittarius in love

In love, Sagittarius needs a partner who is also a fellow traveler: honesty, laughter, and room to roam inside the bond. Sagittarius needs freedom, truth told plainly, and a life that keeps growing.

Where this pair is strong

Fire feels believed in, and earth feels genuinely alive; each gives the other the ingredient they run short of alone.

Where it rubs

Pace is the friction: fire wants it now, earth wants it right. Rushing the earth sign or stalling the fire sign both breed quiet resentment.

Virgo withdraws to think when overwhelmed or feeling criticized; the distance is analysis, not absence of feeling.

Feeling trapped is the fastest way to make a Sagittarius disappear; distance from them usually means the walls felt like they were closing in.

Both are mutable signs, so they move at a similar rhythm and butt heads in similar places.
