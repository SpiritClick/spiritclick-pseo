The Leo side of it

Warm, proud, and expressive, generous with affection and noticeably dimmed by being ignored. Wounded pride goes quiet. A Leo who stops performing for you is often nursing a hurt they will not name first.

Leo needs appreciation, honesty, and to feel chosen out loud, not just in private.

What Six of Cups says here

In love, the Six of Cups can bring an old flame or an old friend back into view, or simply a sweeter, simpler phase with a partner. Judge the present person, not the memory.

The Six of Cups carries the warmth of memory. It can bring reunions, old friends resurfacing, or simple pleasures that remind you of who you were before life got complicated. Kindness, given and received plainly, is the currency of this card.

Advice: Take the good memory with you and keep walking forward.

What Judgement says here

In love, Judgement favors honest stock-taking, forgiveness where it is earned, and deciding whether a relationship gets a genuine fresh start. Old scores kept in a drawer help no one.

Judgement is the moment of honest review. It asks you to look at the whole story, yours included, without flinching or exaggerating, and then rise to what it asks of you. Many people meet this card when a real calling or a big decision can no longer be postponed.

Advice: Tell yourself the whole truth, kindly, and act on it.
